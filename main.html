Origin Lore & The Cosmic Front

Awakening on Pulau Rintis

During a holiday visit to Tok Aba's cocoa stall, BoBoiBoy awakened Power Sphera Ochobot. Cocoa beans held concentrated cosmic energy, attracting alien warlord Adu Du. Ochobot gifted BoBoiBoy elemental powers to protect Earth.
