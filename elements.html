The 7 Elemental Powers

Through his bond with Power Sphera Ochobot, BoBoiBoy commands seven primal forces across three evolutionary stages.

7 Primal ForcesTier 1 & Tier 2 Masteries4 Elemental Fusions

3-Stage Elemental Progression

  1. Stage 1 — Base Awakenings (Tier 1): Primal forces (Petir, Angin, Tanah, Api, Air, Daun, Cahaya) enabling environmental manipulation and Kuasa Tiga.
  2. Stage 2 — Evolved Masteries (Tier 2): Awakened through willpower (Halilintar, Taufan, Gempa, Blaze, Ais, Duri, Solar) with weapons and Kuasa Tujuh.
  3. Stage 3 — Dual Fusions (Tier 3): Synthesizes two elemental forms into a transcendent hybrid warrior (FrostFire, Glacier, Supra, SORI).
