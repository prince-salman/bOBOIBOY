The 7 Elemental Powers

Granted by Power Sphera Ochobot, BoBoiBoy commands seven primal forces of nature. Through discipline and courage, he unlocks advanced second-tier masteries and dual fusions.

Elemental Evolution Stages

How BoBoiBoy progresses his powers through three distinct development tiers.

  1. Stage 1 (Base Forms): Awakened on Earth. Includes Petir (Lightning), Angin (Wind), Tanah (Earth), Api (Fire), Air (Water), Daun (Leaf), and Cahaya (Light).
  2. Stage 2 (Evolved Forms): Unlocked through emotional focus and intense combat. Upgraded to Halilintar, Taufan, Gempa, Blaze, Ais, Duri, and Solar with specialized weapons.
  3. Stage 3 (Dual Fusions): First revealed in BoBoiBoy Movie 2. Merges two compatible elements simultaneously into a single advanced form like FrostFire or Supra.

The 7 Primary Elements

Details on each individual power branch and signature weapon.

Speed • Petir

Halilintar (Lightning)

Delivers lightning-fast dashes and cuts through obstacles with twin red lightning blades (Pedang Halilintar).

Agility • Angin

Taufan (Wind)

Commands atmospheric currents and rides a high-velocity hoverboard to launch devastating air vortexes.

Defense • Tanah

Gempa (Earth)

Forms impenetrable stone barriers and summons giant rock golems to defend allies against heavy artillery.

Offense • Api

Blaze (Fire)

Unleashes fiery explosions and throws twin flaming chakrams that incinerate alien armor at close or mid range.

Cryo • Air

Ais (Ice)

Freezes targets into solid permafrost using a precision crystalline cryo bow to neutralize charging enemies.

Nature • Daun

Duri (Thorn)

Controls thorny vines and bio-organic roots to entangle opponents and set traps across complex terrain.

Solar • Cahaya

Solar (Light)

Concentrates pure solar energy into high-caliber optical blasts and devastating orbital laser cannons.

Movie 2 Dual Fusions

Combinations where two distinct elemental powers merge into one form.

BoBoiBoy FrostFire

Blaze + Ais Fusion

Combines thermal fire chakrams with sub-zero cryo arrows to produce extreme thermal shockwaves against Retak'ka.

BoBoiBoy Glacier

Ais + Gempa Fusion

A massive armored titan constructed of permafrost and granite, wielding giant ice warhammers for heavy defense.

BoBoiBoy Supra

Solar + Halilintar Fusion

An apex warrior wielding solar-plasma blades with instantaneous lightning teleportation speed.

BoBoiBoy SORI

Solar + Duri Fusion

Featured in BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 2, wielding thorny flails charged with blinding solar energy.
