The 7 Primal Elemental Powers

Through Power Sphera Ochobot, BoBoiBoy commands seven elements across base, evolved, and dual fusion tiers.

3 Stages of Evolution

  1. Stage 1 (Base): Petir, Angin, Tanah, Api, Air, Daun, Cahaya.
  2. Stage 2 (Evolved): Halilintar, Taufan, Gempa, Blaze, Ais, Duri, Solar.
  3. Stage 3 (Dual Fusion): FrostFire, Glacier, Supra, SORI, Gentar.
