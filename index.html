The Origin on Pulau Rintis

BoBoiBoy came to Pulau Rintis to spend holidays with his grandfather Tok Aba. Upon awakening the robot Ochobot, he received superpowers to protect the town from alien cocoa thieves.

Interstellar Missions

Together with Yaya, Ying, Gopal, and Fang, BoBoiBoy joined Commander Koko Ci at TAPOPS to save endangered Power Spheras across distant galaxies.
