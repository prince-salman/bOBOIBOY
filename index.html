Franchise Overview

Key pillars that make the BoBoiBoy storyline beloved across Southeast Asia and the globe.

Elemental Mastery

7 Primal Forces

BoBoiBoy commands seven elements: Lightning, Wind, Earth, Fire, Water, Leaf, and Light, evolving them through training and willpower.

TAPOPS Alliance

Space Guardians

Joining the Tracker and Protector of Power Spheras organization, the team travels across galaxies to rescue sentient robotic artifacts.

Cinematic Universe

Blockbuster Films

Two major theatrical films expanded the lore, introducing elemental fusion warriors and ancient cosmic tyrants like Retak'ka.

The Story So Far

From humble beginnings on Pulau Rintis to epic interstellar space defense.

The Cocoa Stall Awakening

BoBoiBoy originally arrived at Pulau Rintis during school holidays to help his grandfather, Tok Aba, at his famous cocoa stall. After awakening Power Sphera Ochobot, BoBoiBoy was granted elemental abilities to thwart alien invaders seeking Tok Aba's cocoa beans as a source of energy.

Intergalactic Missions with TAPOPS

As alien threats grew beyond Earth, Commander Koko Ci recruited BoBoiBoy, Yaya, Ying, Gopal, and Fang into the TAPOPS space academy. Operating from mobile spaceships and orbital stations, the young cadets face galactic armadas to safeguard universal harmony.
