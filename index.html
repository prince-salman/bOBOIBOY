The Tok Aba Cocoa Outpost

BoBoiBoy's journey began on Earth when he visited his grandfather Tok Aba on Pulau Rintis. Upon awakening Power Sphera Ochobot, BoBoiBoy was granted the extraordinary power to manipulate Lightning, Wind, and Earth.

Through unwavering courage, he learned to split into three distinct elemental manifestations simultaneously via Kuasa Tiga.

TAPOPS Interstellar Commission

Recruited into TAPOPS (Tracker and Protector of Power Spheras) under Commander Koko Ci, BoBoiBoy and his trusted comrades journey across hostile galaxies.

Together, they recover endangered robotic Power Spheras, unlock advanced Tier 2 masteries, and achieve transcendent dual-element fusions against galactic conquerors.
