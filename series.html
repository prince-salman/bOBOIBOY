Series & Feature Films

Follow the production history of the franchise by Animonsta Studios, spanning the classic Pulau Rintis television seasons, the Galaxy space saga, and theatrical films.

Major Theatrical Releases

Record-breaking animated movies released in cinemas across Southeast Asia.

BoBoiBoy: The Movie

Released 2016 • 100 Mins

BoBoiBoy and his friends venture to the mysterious Floating Island to rescue Power Sphera Klamkabot from the Tengkotak pirate armada, unlocking BoBoiBoy Fire and Water.

BoBoiBoy Movie 2

Released 2019 • 117 Mins

Ancient master Retak'ka awakens to reclaim all seven elemental powers. BoBoiBoy undergoes rigorous planetary training and achieves unprecedented dual elemental fusions.

Galaxy Season 2

Broadcast 2023+ • TV Arc

The ongoing continuation of the Galaxy storyline exploring Planet Windara and Planet Baraju, introducing new threats and new fusion warriors like BoBoiBoy SORI.

Chronological Watch Order

The recommended order to experience the storyline from start to finish.

  1. BoBoiBoy Classic (Seasons 1–3, 2011–2016): Introduction to BoBoiBoy, his grandfather Tok Aba, and the first awakening of the base elements against Adu Du.
  2. BoBoiBoy: The Movie (2016): The first cinematic adventure introducing Klamkabot and the evolutions of BoBoiBoy Blaze and Ais.
  3. BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 1 (2016–2018): The shift to space exploration with TAPOPS, unveiling BoBoiBoy Leaf and Solar across 24 episodes.
  4. BoBoiBoy Movie 2 (2019): The high-stakes clash against Retak'ka, leading to the breakthrough of dual elemental fusions.
  5. BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 2 (2023–Present): The ongoing Windara and Baraju campaigns exploring elemental lore and ancient kingdoms.

Studio & Production Details

Key background facts about the creation and studio behind the franchise.

  • Animation Studio: Animonsta Studios (operating as Monsta), based in Cyberjaya, Malaysia.
  • Series Creator & Director: Nizam Razak, who co-founded Monsta in 2009.
  • Broadcast Reach: Aired in over 70 countries and translated into dozens of regional languages.
  • Cultural Impact: Promotes values of teamwork, friendship, multicultural harmony, and environmental responsibility.
