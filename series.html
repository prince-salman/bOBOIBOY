Official Canon Chronological Watch Order

  1. BoBoiBoy Classic (Seasons 1–3): Awakening on Pulau Rintis, meeting Ochobot, and discovering the original elemental powers (Petir, Angin, Tanah, Api, Air).

  5. BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 2 (Windara & Beyond): Planetary liberation campaigns and advanced fusions SORI and Gentar.
