BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 1: TAPOPS Space Expeditions & The 7 Elements

Venturing far beyond Earth's atmosphere, BoBoiBoy alongside Gopal, Yaya, Ying, and Fang are recruited as cadets into TAPOPS (Tracker and Protector of Power Spheras) under the leadership of Commander Koko Ci and Admiral Tarung. Aboard the Sunnova starship, these young heroes traverse the cosmos to safeguard stranded Power Spheras before galactic criminal syndicates exploit their powers.

Main Conflict: Feared space pirate Captain Vargoba, master of Dark Magnetism, launches a devastating assault on the TAPOPS Space Station. With his teammates incapacitated and station shields shattered, BoBoiBoy must fight alone in deep space and awaken the ultimate form of the seventh element to save the galaxy.

⚡ Elemental Milestones & Key Breakthroughs

  • Awakening of Leaf & Thorn: BoBoiBoy awakens the gentle Leaf element on Planet Gurunda, which later evolves into the deadly thorn-whip specialist BoBoiBoy Thorn.
  • Awakening of Light & Solar: The seventh and final primal element Light is unlocked, rapidly evolving into the apex form BoBoiBoy Solar during the climactic duel.
  • Solar Maximum Shot: The most devastating beam cannon attack in the TV series, obliterating Captain Vargoba's gravitational black hole singularity.
Primary Antagonists: Captain Vargoba Jugglenaut Panto Roktaroka Jokertu Adu Du (Bounty Hunter)

BoBoiBoy Movie 2: Wrath of Retak'ka & The Birth of Elemental Fusions

Retak'ka, an ancient cosmic tyrant from millennia past and original wielder of the seven elemental powers, escapes from an asteroid crystal prison. Seeking to reclaim his stolen dominion from BoBoiBoy, Retak'ka hunts down and forcibly extracts four core elements (Halilintar, Solar, Gempa, Taufan). Depleted and desperate, BoBoiBoy retreats to Planet Quabaq to train under legendary master Hang Kasa.

Main Conflict: Retak'ka evolves into a godlike titan on Planet Quabaq. Facing the brink of extinction and Ochobot's ultimate sacrifice, BoBoiBoy transcends his classic clone-splitting technique to invent simultaneous dual-elemental synthesis (Elemental Fusion)—an unprecedented milestone in cosmic history.

⚡ Elemental Milestones & Key Breakthroughs

  • BoBoiBoy FrostFire (Fire + Ice): First recorded dual-fusion, synthesizing volcanic lava blasts with absolute-zero arctic shockwaves.
  • BoBoiBoy Glacier (Ice + Earthquake): Colossal titan fusion wielding glacial crystal constructs and impenetrable tectonic shields.
  • BoBoiBoy Supra (Solar + Thunderstorm): The ultimate fusion warrior wielding dual supersonic light-lance strikes that shattered Retak'ka's armor.
Primary Antagonists: Retak'ka (Voltra / Gamma / Beliung / Balak) Captain Separo Mining Scavenger Guild

BoBoiBoy Galaxy Season 2: The Planetary Knights & Windara Arc

Following Retak'ka's fall, the balance of power across the cosmos undergoes seismic shifts. Ancient elemental empires re-emerge. In the Windara Arc, BoBoiBoy and his vanguard journey to Planet Windara—a majestic skyborne realm suspended above eternal storm clouds—to help legitimate Empress Kuputeri reclaim her rightful throne and Wind emblem from usurper Emperor Reramos.

Main Conflict: Emperor Reramos mind-controls planetary citizens and guards using hypnotic flower spores while bolstering his legions with synthetic wind armor. Meanwhile, a subterranean swarm of giant beetles threatens to collapse the planetary root pillars of Windara.

⚡ Elemental Milestones & Key Breakthroughs

  • BoBoiBoy SORI (Solar + Thorn): An agile tactical fusion armed with solar-charged thorned flails capable of blinding, rapid strikes.
  • BoBoiBoy Gentar (Earthquake + Thunderstorm): A heavyweight tectonic brute wielding dual seismic warhammers supercharged with high-voltage electricity.
  • Tier 3 Elemental Resonance: Foreshadowing BoBoiBoy Beliung (Tier 3 Wind Evolution) aligning in harmony with Kuputeri's royal crest.
Primary Antagonists: Emperor Reramos General Maripos (Rival turned Ally) Windara Beetle Swarm Ancient Kadrux Cult

Cadet Viewing FAQ & Quick Tips

Can I start with BoBoiBoy Galaxy?

Yes! BoBoiBoy Galaxy acts as a soft franchise reboot designed to welcome new international viewers. However, watching BoBoiBoy: The Movie (2016) first provides crucial context regarding Klamkabot and Ochobot's upgrades.

Are the Movies Official Canon?

100% Yes. Unlike anime feature films that are often non-canonical spin-offs, Monsta's movies are primary milestones that permanently advance the narrative, introduce new tier awakenings, and introduce pivotal characters like Hang Kasa.

Evolution vs. Elemental Fusion?

Evolution (Tier 2) is the upgrade of a single element (e.g. Petir → Halilintar). Fusion merges two distinct elemental spheres simultaneously into a unified warrior (e.g. Fire + Ice = FrostFire; Solar + Halilintar = Supra).
