Heroes of the Galaxy

Meet the young protectors who defend Power Spheras from dangerous warlords across the cosmos, each wielding distinct superhuman abilities granted by Ochobot.

Core TAPOPS Squad

The frontline team defending peace across Earth and alien planets.

Squad Leader

BoBoiBoy

Courageous superhero wielding seven primal elements. He can split into three or seven independent clones simultaneously to fight multiple enemies at once.

  • Abilities: 7 Elements • Clone Division
  • Signature: Kuasa Tiga • Kuasa Tujuh
  • Origin: Earth (Pulau Rintis)
Gravity Master

Yaya

A principled student leader who commands gravity manipulation. She can fly at supersonic velocities and deliver devastating gravitational strikes in combat.

  • Abilities: Flight • Gravity Manipulation
  • Signature: Tumbukan Padu Graviti
  • Origin: Earth (Pulau Rintis)
Time Speedster

Ying

An energetic athlete who controls localized time dilation. She can accelerate her own movement to extreme speeds while slowing down incoming enemy attacks.

  • Abilities: Time Acceleration • Deceleration
  • Signature: Larian Laju Masa
  • Origin: Earth (Pulau Rintis)
Matter Transmuter

Gopal

BoBoiBoy's fun-loving best friend who can transmute any physical object into food. He often turns lethal enemy projectiles and metal traps into snacks.

  • Abilities: Molecular Food Transmutation
  • Signature: Tukaran Makanan
  • Origin: Earth (Pulau Rintis)
Shadow Specialist

Fang

A competitive rival turned reliable ally and TAPOPS captain. Fang shapes living darkness into giant shadow eagles, tigers, and impenetrable defensive shields.

  • Abilities: Shadow Energy Manipulation
  • Signature: Helang Bayang • Harimau Bayang
  • Origin: Planet Gurlatan
Generation 9 Sphera

Ochobot

The friendly yellow robot who awakened the superpowers of BoBoiBoy and his friends. He manages spatial warp portals and provides tactical mission telemetry.

  • Abilities: Spatial Teleportation • Energy Channeling
  • Signature: Kuasa Teleportasi Dimensi
  • Origin: Ancient Power Sphera Laboratory

Allies & Mentors

Influential figures who guide and support the young heroes.

Tok Aba

Grandfather • Cocoa Master

BoBoiBoy's wise and supportive grandfather who owns the Kokotiam stall on Pulau Rintis. He offers warm advice, moral support, and famous hot chocolate recipes.

Papa Zola

Teacher • Academy Instructor

An eccentric school teacher with a dramatic flair for justice. Despite his humorous antics, he cares deeply about his students and helps train them at the TAPOPS academy.
