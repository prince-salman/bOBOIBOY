Elemental Superhero & Clone Master

Earth's premier defender and the tactical anchor of the TAPOPS squad. BoBoiBoy wields the unprecedented gift of commanding seven primal elements — Lightning, Wind, Earth, Fire, Water, Leaf, and Light. Using his signature BoBoiBoy Kuasa Tiga and Kuasa Tujuh maneuvers, he splits his consciousness into separate elemental clones, multiplying his tactical presence across any cosmic battlefield.

Yaya

Gravity Manipulation & Super Strength

Disciplined, upright, and fiercely courageous. Yaya can manipulate gravitational vectors, allowing her to soar at supersonic flight altitudes and strike with immense physical power. She can alter the mass of enemy armor, immobilize titanic alien mechs in midair, or smash through blast doors with her Tumbukan Padu. Outside of combat, she is a dedicated student council leader famous for her notoriously indestructible biscuits.

Ying

Time Acceleration & Temporal Reflexes

The fastest heroine in the galaxy. Ying commands temporal manipulation, allowing her to accelerate the flow of time around her body to reach hypersonic speeds. She weaves effortlessly through volleys of plasma fire, delivers blinding multi-kick combinations via Tendangan Laju, and executes rapid battlefield evacuations before adversaries even register her movement. In combat, she and Yaya form an unstoppable complementary assault duo.

Gopal

Molecular Transmutation & Heavy Support

BoBoiBoy's warm-hearted childhood friend who wields one of the most game-changing superpowers in existence: atomic molecular restructuring. With a touch or ray burst, Gopal can convert high-yield alien artillery, laser missiles, and dense titanium shields into delicious candy, chocolate bars, and curry puffs. While he initially flees from danger, his panic-induced transmutations have repeatedly saved TAPOPS from catastrophic destruction.

Mentors & Beloved Allies

Behind every victorious galactic battle stands the invaluable wisdom, warm cocoa, and heroic motivation of Earth's finest mentors.

Pulau Rintis Guardian

Tok Aba

Kokotiam Master & Grandfather

BoBoiBoy's wise, warm-hearted grandfather and master proprietor of Tok Aba's Kokotiam on Pulau Rintis. Unbeknownst to anyone at first, his secret special hot cocoa recipe held the precise energetic frequency required to revive Ochobot from his centuries-long deep slumber.

Whenever galactic battles wear heavy on the young squad, Tok Aba provides steady grounding, emotional reassurance, and life wisdom. He reminds BoBoiBoy that true strength does not merely lie in cosmic fire or thunder, but in protecting the bonds of friendship and family.

Signature Saying

"Tok Aba's special hot cocoa isn't just ordinary chocolate—it delivers invigorating energy and warmth to anyone who tastes it!"

— Tok Aba, Founder of Tok Aba's Kokotiam

Headquarters: Tok Aba Kokotiam, Earth Specialty: Cocoa Energy
Champion of Justice

Papa Zola

TAPOPS Academy Instructor & Educator

The flamboyant, indomitable school teacher who claims the grand title of "Hero of Justice". After accidentally being pulled into cosmic escapades alongside his students, Papa Zola was appointed as TAPOPS Academy's physical education mentor and motivational instructor.

While frequently delivering comically exaggerated lectures and boasting of his invincible gaming prowess, Papa Zola possesses true valor. When alien forces threaten his beloved pupils, Papa Zola fearlessly steps into the fray, brandishing pure bravado and unbreakable conviction.

Battle Proclamation

"Justice shall never waver! Prepare yourselves to face the greatness and righteousness of Papa Zola, you cosmic scoundrels!"

— Papa Zola, Hero of Justice & Mathematics
