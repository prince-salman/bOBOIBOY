Frequently Asked Questions

Essential knowledge regarding TAPOPS peacekeeping, Power Spheras, and site engineering.

What is TAPOPS and what is its galactic mission?

TAPOPS (Tracker and Protector of Power Spheras) is an interstellar defense coalition tasked with locating, securing, and safeguarding ancient Power Spheras before they are exploited by mercenary warlords and space conquerors.

What is the difference between Evolutions and Elemental Fusions?

Second-Tier Evolutions (Halilintar, Taufan, Gempa, Blaze, Ice, Duri, Solar) represent concentrated mastery of a single element. Elemental Fusions (FrostFire, Glacier, Supra, SORI) merge two distinct elemental spheres simultaneously into a unified hybrid warrior with exponential power.

How is this website engineered with pure semantic HTML5 & CSS3?

The site uses standard semantic HTML5 elements, pure CSS mobile navigation drawer via the hidden checkbox pattern, accessible form validation, and CSS custom property design tokens without any external JavaScript dependencies.
