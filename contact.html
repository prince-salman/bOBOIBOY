Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the franchise and our portal.

Who created BoBoiBoy and when did it premiere?

BoBoiBoy was created by Nizam Razak and produced by Animonsta Studios (Monsta) in Malaysia. The original series premiered in March 2011.

What is the difference between original BoBoiBoy and Galaxy?

The original series took place primarily on Earth around Pulau Rintis. BoBoiBoy Galaxy follows the characters as teenage cadets exploring space stations and alien planets with TAPOPS.

How do Elemental Fusions work?

Elemental Fusions occur when BoBoiBoy synchronizes two distinct elements into a single hybrid form (e.g., Blaze and Ice into FrostFire), providing unprecedented power against high-level opponents.

Where can I legally watch official BoBoiBoy episodes?

Episodes and films are available on Monsta's official YouTube channel, major streaming platforms such as Netflix, and regional television networks across Southeast Asia.
