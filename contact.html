Frequently Asked Questions

What is TAPOPS?

TAPOPS (Tracker And Protector of Power Spheras) is a galactic organization dedicated to safeguarding robotic Power Spheras from evil entities across the cosmos.

How does BoBoiBoy command 7 elements?

BoBoiBoy was granted elemental powers by Power Sphera Ochobot. He splits into clones to master Lightning, Wind, Earth, Fire, Water, Leaf, and Light.

What are Elemental Fusions?

Introduced in BoBoiBoy Movie 2, fusions combine two distinct elemental forms into a single high-tier manifestation such as FrostFire, Glacier, Supra, and SORI.
